How a study runs with us

One accredited lab, from the sample to publication.

You keep the study design. We handle each stage in-house and stay involved through to your paper. The advantage at every stage is that one team is accountable for all of it.

01
Onboarding and samples
You send samples, or we help you collect them with room-temperature kits, so there is no cold chain to manage. You do not have to generate any data yourself.
Key point: every sample is tracked on a dashboard, so you can see where each one is.
02
Extraction and sequencing
DNA extraction and long-read shotgun sequencing, run in-house. Reads are classified against an in-house reference of about 250,000 genomes.
Key point: species-level resolution, and strain level where the data allows.
03
Analysis
Five to six kinds of analysis on every study: composition, alpha and beta diversity, core microbiome, differential abundance across several methods, and function.
Key point: the full analysis suite, not a single test, so findings hold up to review.
04
Interpretation
We read the results against your question and write up what they mean. You do not receive a folder of files to work through on your own.
Key point: results are interpreted, not just delivered.
05
Publication
We stay on through analysis revisions, figures and reviewer questions, up to publication.
Key point: support continues after the report is delivered.

Why the method matters

Three things that decide whether microbiome findings hold up.

01

Sequencing resolution

16S amplicon sequencing resolves communities to genus. Species identification and function need shotgun metagenomics.

02

Statistical method

Differential-abundance methods often disagree. A taxon significant under one method may not hold under another, so we run several and report each.

03

One accountable lab

When sequencing, analysis and figures are split across vendors, no single party is responsible and sources of variation are harder to control.

Inside the lab

Run in-house, end to end.

Sequencing, extraction and QC happen under one accredited roof, so there is no handoff between vendors.

Sequencing

Long-read shotgun metagenomics

Whole-metagenome long-read sequencing, resolved to species level.

DNA extraction

Automated magnetic-bead extraction

Genomic DNA extracted from stool and other sample types on an automated magnetic-bead platform.

Quantification & QC

Fluorometric and spectrophotometric QC

DNA is quantified on a Qubit fluorometer and checked for purity on a NanoDrop spectrophotometer; reads are then quality-filtered and host sequences removed.

Analysis

In-house MetaRich pipeline

Host-read removal against the human genome, then Kraken2 classification against a curated NCBI RefSeq reference.

Data and confidentiality

Your samples, data and IP stay yours.

Your samples, sequence data and results remain your property. We work under an NDA, and the terms of handling, data retention and deletion are set out in the MOU signed before any samples move.

Handling is aligned with India's DPDP Act and with GDPR. Access is restricted, chain of custody is kept end to end, and nothing is reused for any other purpose. Retention and deletion can be defined to your requirement and discussed at the scoping call.

Frequently asked

The questions researchers ask first.

Can you analyse data we have already sequenced?

Yes. You can start at any stage. Send raw reads and we run the full analysis and interpretation. If you already have samples or extracted DNA, we pick up from there instead.

Do we have to start from the sample?

No. You can join at samples, extracted DNA, sequence data or analysis output. We continue from whatever you already have.

Why long-read shotgun instead of 16S?

16S stops at genus and does not resolve function. Long-read shotgun metagenomics reaches species level and gives functional pathways and the resistome.

How long does a study take?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks from receipt of the last sample, depending on sample count and the analyses requested. Cohorts are sequenced together to reduce batch effects.

How do samples ship, do they need cold chain?

No. Samples are collected into a nucleic-acid preservative that keeps them stable at room temperature, so batches ship together without a cold chain.

Who owns the data and results?

You do. Handling is aligned with the DPDP Act and GDPR, under an NDA and MOU. Your samples, sequences and IP are never reused.
