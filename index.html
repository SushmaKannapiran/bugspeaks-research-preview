Method

Three factors that determine whether microbiome findings replicate.

01

Sequencing resolution

16S amplicon sequencing resolves communities to genus level. Species identification and functional profiling require shotgun metagenomics.

02

Statistical method

Differential-abundance methods often disagree. A taxon significant under one method may not hold under another, so single-method results are difficult to defend.

03

Chain of custody

When sequencing, analysis and figures are split across vendors, no single party is accountable for the study and sources of variation are harder to control.

Species-level resolution, statistics that hold across methods, and a single accredited lab responsible for the whole study.

Differential abundance

Six methods, reported in full, with a consensus layer.

Each of the six differential-abundance methods is run and its complete output is delivered. A consensus layer identifies the taxa found significant across methods, at the p-value and FDR thresholds you set.

Frequently asked

The questions researchers ask first.

Why long-read shotgun instead of 16S?

16S stops at genus and can't resolve function; our long-read shotgun metagenomics reaches species level and gives you functional pathways and the resistome.

Why run the full six-method differential suite?

Because a single method is a reviewer's easiest objection. We deliver all six methods' complete results and flag what's robust across them, and you set the thresholds.

How long does a study take?

Typically 2 to 3 weeks from sample receipt to delivered figures and tables. Cohorts are sequenced together at study end to reduce batch effects.

How do samples ship, do they need cold chain?

No. Our barcoded kits stabilise samples at room temperature for up to 15 days, so batches ship together at ambient temperature.

Who owns the data and results?

You do. DPDP- and GDPR-aligned; your samples, sequences and IP are never repurposed.

Are you accredited?

NABL (ISO 15189:2022), ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and NABH, the only microbiome company in Asia accredited across these standards. Certificate numbers on request.
